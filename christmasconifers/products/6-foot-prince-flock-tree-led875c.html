    Size Guide

    Choosing the Perfect Height

    Aim for approximately 6 inches of space between the ceiling and the top of your tree (or tree topper). All heights include tree stands.

    Under 6'

    Great for tabletops, fireplaces, kitchens, dens, etc.

    6’ – 6.5’

    Ideal for homes with low to average ceiling height.

    7’ – 7.5’

    Perfect for average homes with 8’ to 9’ ceiling heights.

    8’ – 9’

    Suitable for rooms with 10’ to 11’ ceiling heights.

    10’ – 12’

    Optimal for 2-story foyers, living rooms, and other areas with very high ceiling heights.

    Chooses Shape and Style

    Express your unique style with tree shapes for every taste, and every space. Allow about 12 inches of space beyond the width of your tree in all directions for optimal setup. Short or tall, slim or wide, we have the perfectly handcrafted tree for any space.

    Wide

    Our most expansive tree, offering generous room for your largest ornament collections.

    Standard (Full)

    Your typical tree design, featuring the perfect blend of sturdiness and unrivaled  beauty.

    Sparse

    A sporadic design that ensures your ornaments and custom touches stand out above all.

    Slim

    When space is tight or you desire a minimalistic look, these slender profiles check the box.

    Technology

    Power Pole

    Designed with an integrated Power Pole, each tree connects effortlessly. Simply stack the sections, plug in, and enjoy!

    • The height includes the stand and is measured from the floor to the tallest tip of the assembled tree. The width is measured from one end to the other at the widest point of the fully assembled tree (diameter).

    • That depends on how the tree is shaped, the bottom branches will be approximately 9"-14" inches from the floor.

    • Yes, all our trees are reinforced with steel and are outfitted to hold heavy ornaments.

    • The lights on our pre-lit trees are professionally hand-strung and securely attached to the branches for an even, seamless look and easy setup.

    • We use premium Micro LED lights designed with a life expectancy of up to 25,000 hours.

    • A Power Pole is a built-in lighting connection system that automatically connects the lights between each section of the tree during setup, eliminating the need to plug in individual light wires manually.

    • Our flocking is made from a soft, cotton-based material that gives the tree its realistic snow-covered appearance and is designed for safe indoor use around families and pets.

    • The King and Queen trees feature a blend of PE and PVC tips for a more realistic look and fullness, while the Prince tree is made with 100% PVC tips. Queen trees also have a wider diameter and feature triple-headed tips for a fuller appearance, whereas King trees use single-headed tips.
