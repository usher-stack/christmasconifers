    The holiday season is a joyous time of year for everyone, but the shopping part can be a little stressful at times. With the Tribeca Spruce Collection Set, decorating your home will be a breeze. The Tribeca Spruce Collection perfectly complements our Tribeca Spruce Tree. This set features two beautiful 3' pre-lit entryway trees, a pre-lit flexible 9'x12" garland that can be used to wrap around a banister, hang around doorpost entrance, or drape over a fireplace mantel, and a charming pre-lit wreath to complete the set. This collection set is battery operated with a built-in timer setting of 6 hours on and 18 hours off.

    • Each Collection Set includes:
      2 Battery-Operated Tabletop Trees
      1 Battery-Operated Wreath
      1 Battery-Operated Garland

    • Each battery-operated item requires 4 D batteries. Since the Collection Set includes four battery-operated pieces, you'll need 16 D batteries in total if you plan to use all items at the same time.

    • Yes! Each battery-operated item features a built-in timer. Press the ON/OFF button once to activate the timer, which keeps the lights on for 6 hours and off for 18 hours, automatically repeating every 24 hours.

    • Yes. Press the ON/OFF button a second time to keep the lights on continuously. Press it a third time to turn the lights off.

    • Yes, partially. The two tabletop trees and garland can be powered using either 4 D batteries or the included LED plug-in adapters, so they can be plugged into a standard wall outlet. The wreath is battery operated only and cannot be plugged in.
